About this demo

A portfolio project that digitises the paper-based protocol of a basic chemistry practical: validated entry at the bench, automatic calculation, comparison with the class and rule-based hints on likely errors.

How the checks work

  • Entry checks block impossible values (end reading below start, mass in milligrams, readings above the burette capacity) and warn about unusual ones.
  • Class comparison uses the median and the median absolute deviation, so a few wrong values do not shift the reference.
  • Station check compares the median of each lab station with the class.
  • Typo check tests whether swapping two digits of one reading makes a titration agree with the other two.
  • No AI model is used in this version. Every finding names the rule behind it.

Privacy

  • The demo contains only synthetic data. Group codes are pseudonyms; no names are collected.
  • No cookies, no tracking, no external fonts or scripts.
  • If deployed publicly, add an Impressum and a privacy notice (server logs) before going live.

Safety and scope

This is a software demo, not an official lab instruction. Experiments are carried out only under the supervision of the lab staff and according to the safety data sheets. The app gives feedback only and does not grade.
